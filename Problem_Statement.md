## 1. Title

**AI-Based Interview & Placement Preparation Portal**

## 2. Domain

**Education Technology (EdTech) & Artificial Intelligence**

* Attend mock interviews
* Upload resume
* Receive AI-based feedback

* Add interview questions
* Conduct mock interviews

### Admin

* Manage students and trainers
* Manage companies
* View placement analytics
* Manage the complete portal

## 4. Problem Statement

Students often use multiple websites and applications for aptitude practice, coding preparation, resume building, and interview preparation. Since these resources are scattered across different platforms, students find it difficult to organize their preparation, monitor their progress, and identify their weak areas.

The **AI-Based Interview & Placement Preparation Portal** provides a single platform for students to prepare for placements through aptitude tests, coding challenges, resume analysis, and mock interviews. AI can analyze resumes and interview answers and provide personalized feedback to help students improve their placement readiness.
